# Support

Open the right channel and your question gets answered faster.

| Need | Where |
|---|---|
| A lab does not work as documented | [Lab bug](https://github.com/duckurity/openlabs/issues/new?template=lab-bug.yml) issue form |
| An idea for a lab | [Lab idea](https://github.com/duckurity/openlabs/issues/new?template=lab-idea.yml) issue form |
| Questions about solving or setup | [Discussions](https://github.com/duckurity/openlabs/discussions) |
| A vulnerability | [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), private report only |

For everything else, open a discussion. Public answers help the next person
who hits the same wall.
