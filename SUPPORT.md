# Support

Open the right channel and your question gets answered faster.

| Need | Where |
|---|---|
| A lab does not work as documented | [Lab bug](https://github.com/duckurity/openlabs/issues/new?template=lab-bug.yml) issue form |
| An idea for a lab | [Lab idea](https://github.com/duckurity/openlabs/issues/new?template=lab-idea.yml) issue form |
| Questions about solving or setup | [Question](https://github.com/duckurity/openlabs/issues/new?template=question.yml) issue form |
| A vulnerability in repo tooling or lab infrastructure | [Private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/duckurity/openlabs/security/advisories/new) ([SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) |

Do not paste flags, tokens, or secrets in public issues. Redact host paths
that identify your machine.

For lab design questions and general help, prefer the question form so others
can find the thread later.
