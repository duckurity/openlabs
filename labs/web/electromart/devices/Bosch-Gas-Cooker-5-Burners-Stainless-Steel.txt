DESCRIPTION
==================================================

The Bosch HGX3G8W50S is a premium freestanding gas cooker
with five burners and a stainless steel exterior. It is
designed for everyday cooking while providing additional
features for oven and grilling tasks.

The cooker features five gas burners with cast iron holders,
providing a stable surface for different sizes of cookware.
It also includes automatic ignition for easier burner
operation.

The oven includes a double grill system and an oven light,
allowing users to monitor food while cooking. The glass oven
door provides visibility while helping retain heat inside
the oven.

The cooker features a digital display and front-positioned
controls. Bosch also provides a full safety system designed
to improve safety during gas operation.

Its design includes features intended to retain heat and
reduce cooking odors, making it suitable for regular
household cooking and baking.
