DESCRIPTION
==================================================

The LG T1366NEHG2 is a 13kg top-load washing machine
designed for large households and users who regularly wash
large quantities of clothing and bedding.

The machine is powered by an LG Smart Inverter Motor,
which is designed to provide efficient and reliable washing
performance. It also features Smart Motion technology and
TurboDrum to create different washing movements and improve
the cleaning process.

The washing machine features a tempered glass lid and a
middle black exterior. Its LoadSense system can detect the
load and help manage the washing process.

The T1366NEHG2 includes several useful programs such as
Normal, Quick Wash, Pre Wash + Normal, Duvet, Wool,
Extra Clean, Tub Clean, and Silent.

It supports hot, warm, and cold water temperatures and
provides automatic or manual water-level selection. The
machine also provides three spin levels and up to four
rinses.

For convenience, it includes a delay timer from 3 to
19 hours, Auto Restart, Child Lock, End of Cycle Signal,
and Smart Diagnosis.

The machine does not include Wi-Fi, ThinQ remote control,
Steam, or AI Wash features, keeping its operation focused
on physical controls and core washing functions.
