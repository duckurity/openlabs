DESCRIPTION
==================================================

The Google Pixel 10 Pro is a flagship smartphone powered by
the Google Tensor G5 processor built using 3nm technology,
paired with 16GB of RAM.

The phone features a 6.3-inch LTPO OLED display with a
1280 x 2856 resolution, 120Hz refresh rate, HDR10+, and
Corning Gorilla Glass Victus 2 protection. The display can
reach a peak brightness of up to 3300 nits.

The Pixel 10 Pro is available with 128GB, 256GB, 512GB,
or 1TB of internal storage and does not support expandable
storage through a memory card.

The phone features a triple rear camera system consisting of
a 50MP main camera, a 48MP telephoto camera with 5x optical
zoom, and a 48MP ultra-wide camera. The front camera has
a 42MP sensor.

The phone supports video recording up to 8K at 30fps, as well
as 4K recording at up to 60fps. It also includes features such
as Ultra HDR and Best Take for enhanced photography.

The Pixel 10 Pro has a 4870mAh battery supporting 30W wired
fast charging, 15W magnetic Qi2 wireless charging, and
reverse wireless charging.

The phone runs Android 16 and is designed to receive up to
seven major Android upgrades. It supports 5G, Wi-Fi 7,
Bluetooth 6.0, NFC, USB Type-C 3.2, and an ultrasonic
under-display fingerprint sensor.

The device also has an IP68 rating for water and dust
resistance and features stereo speakers.
