DESCRIPTION
==================================================

The iFilter Cold is a countertop smart water filtration system that combines a seven-stage water purification system with an integrated instant cooling function. It is designed to provide purified and chilled drinking water without requiring a separate traditional water dispenser.

The system uses two filter cartridges to provide seven purification stages while keeping the overall design compact. It also features a Smart TDS display that allows users to monitor the water's total dissolved solids level directly from the device.

One of its main features is the integrated cooling tank, which is designed to provide chilled purified water within approximately 6 to 12 minutes. This makes the device suitable for users who want both water filtration and cooling in a single countertop appliance.

The iFilter Cold also includes integrated UV sterilization technology. According to the manufacturer, the UV system is designed to eliminate 99.9% of bacteria and viruses.

For easier maintenance, the device includes an Auto-Flush function for self-cleaning and membrane protection. The internal cooling tank is made from food-grade 304 stainless steel and the system is designed without exposed electrical wiring in contact with water.
