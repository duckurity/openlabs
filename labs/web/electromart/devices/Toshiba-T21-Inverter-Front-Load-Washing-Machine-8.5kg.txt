DESCRIPTION
==================================================

The Toshiba T21 is an 8.5kg front-load inverter washing
machine designed for everyday household laundry. It comes
in a silver finish and combines a compact front-load design
with a variety of washing programs.

The machine features an inverter motor and a maximum spin
speed of 1400 RPM. It provides 16 different washing programs
to accommodate different types of clothing and laundry loads.

Toshiba's GreatWaves washing technology is designed to provide
effective washing while helping protect colors and reduce
energy consumption.

The machine also features an Aroma+ function designed to
increase fragrance efficiency and a UFB function that helps
detergent dissolve and penetrate fabrics more effectively.

A Steam function is included to provide an additional
cleaning option. The washing machine also features a
dedicated tub cleaning cycle to help maintain the interior
of the machine.

The T21 supports Wi-Fi connectivity, allowing it to provide
smart functionality in addition to its physical controls.

With its 8.5kg capacity, 1400 RPM spin speed, 16 programs,
inverter motor, steam function, and Wi-Fi support, the T21
provides a balanced set of features for everyday home use.

Main Specifications:
- Capacity: 8.5 kg

- Motor: Inverter Motor
- Maximum Spin Speed: 1400 RPM
