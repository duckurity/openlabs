DESCRIPTION
==================================================

The Ultra UB60M2WE is a white countertop blender with a
600-watt motor, designed for everyday household blending
and food preparation.

The blender has a 1.6-liter capacity and provides three
speed settings together with a pulse function, allowing
users to select different blending speeds depending on
the ingredients.

It uses stainless steel blades and operates at a frequency
of 50-60Hz. The blender comes with two attachments: a grinder
and a grater, providing additional food preparation options.

Its compact design and relatively low weight make it easy
to place on a kitchen counter and store when not in use.

The UB60M2WE is intended as an affordable multi-function
blender for users who want several speed options and
additional attachments without purchasing a larger appliance.

Main Specifications:
- Brand: Ultra

- Power: 600 Watts
- Capacity: 1.6 Liters
- Speed Settings: 3
- Pulse Function: Yes
- Blades: Stainless Steel
- Number of Attachments: 2
- Attachments: Grinder / Grater
- Frequency: 50-60Hz
- Product Weight: Approximately 1 kg
