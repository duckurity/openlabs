DESCRIPTION
==================================================

The Samsung WW80T4040CX1AS is an 8kg front-load washing
machine designed for everyday household laundry. It features
a silver exterior with a black door and a LED display.

The washing machine is equipped with Samsung's Digital
Inverter Technology motor, providing efficient and reliable
operation. It supports a maximum spin speed of 1400 RPM.

The machine includes a Hygiene Steam cycle that uses steam
to provide a deeper cleaning experience and help remove
allergens from clothes. It also includes intensive washing
and soaking functions for handling more difficult loads.

The washing machine features a 2nd Diamond Drum design,
which is designed to provide a gentler washing environment
for clothes. The StayClean Drawer helps keep the detergent
drawer cleaner by using water to help prevent detergent
residue buildup.

For convenience, the machine includes a 15-minute Quick
Wash program, Pre Wash, Rinse+, Delay End, Drum Clean,
Smart Check, and a Child Lock.

The WW80T4040CX1AS does not include Wi-Fi or SmartThings
connectivity, making it a more straightforward washing
machine focused on traditional controls and washing
performance.
