DESCRIPTION
==================================================

The Fresh Master 17281 is a freestanding four-burner gas
cooker with a silver finish. It combines a standard gas
cooktop with an oven and grill for everyday household
cooking.

The cooker features four gas burners and automatic ignition,
making it convenient to start the burners without using a
separate lighter.

The oven includes an oven light and fan distribution to
help distribute heat during cooking. A dedicated grill
function is also included for grilling and browning food.

The cooker uses front-positioned controls and is designed
as a freestanding appliance. Its 60 x 60 cm dimensions make
it suitable for kitchens where a more compact cooker is
preferred.

The Master 17281 provides a straightforward combination
of cooking, baking, and grilling functions at a more
accessible price point.
