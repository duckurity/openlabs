DESCRIPTION
==================================================

The Unionaire Max Air 11 is a large five-burner gas cooker
with a black finish, designed for everyday cooking, baking,
and grilling.

The cooker features five gas burners with cast iron holders,
providing stable support for pots and pans of different sizes.
It includes automatic ignition for convenient burner
operation.

The oven is equipped with a fan for heat distribution,
helping circulate hot air during cooking. It also includes
an oven light for easier monitoring of food and a grill
function for grilling and browning.

The cooker includes a timer and a safety system. Its
front-positioned controls make the burners and oven
functions easy to access.

The Max Air 11 has dimensions of 85 x 90 x 60 cm and uses
a freestanding installation design, making it suitable for
larger kitchens and households that need five burners.
