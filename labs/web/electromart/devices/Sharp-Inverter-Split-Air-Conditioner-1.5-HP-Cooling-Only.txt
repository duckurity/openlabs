DESCRIPTION
==================================================

The Sharp AH-XP12BME is a 1.5 horsepower inverter split
air conditioner designed for cooling residential spaces
while providing efficient temperature control.

The unit provides a cooling capacity of 12000 BTU and uses
an inverter motor. Its variable-capacity operation is
designed to adjust cooling performance according to the
room's requirements.

The air conditioner features Sharp's Plasmacluster Ion
technology, which is designed to help improve the air
environment. It also includes an anti-dust filter.

The unit includes ECO mode for more efficient operation,
a digital display, a timer, automatic restart, and a Dry
function for reducing humidity.

The AH-XP12BME also supports four-way automatic air swing,
helping distribute cooled air across the room.
