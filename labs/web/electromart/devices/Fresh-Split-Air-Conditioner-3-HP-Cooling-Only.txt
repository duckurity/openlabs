DESCRIPTION
==================================================

The Fresh FUFW24COX is a 3 horsepower split air conditioner
designed for cooling large residential spaces and rooms
that require higher cooling capacity.

The air conditioner provides a cooling capacity of 24000 BTU
and uses a cooling-only configuration. It features an
anti-dust filter designed to help reduce dust entering the
airflow.

Its 3 HP capacity makes it suitable for larger rooms where
a smaller 1.5 HP air conditioner may not provide sufficient
cooling performance.

The unit has a white exterior and uses a split-system design
with separate indoor and outdoor units. Its large cooling
capacity makes it suitable for living rooms, large bedrooms,
offices, and other relatively large spaces.

Main Specifications:
- Brand: Fresh
- Model: FUFW24COX
- Type: Split Air Conditioner
- Cooling Type: Cooling Only
- Horsepower: 3 HP
- Cooling Capacity: 24000 BTU
- Cooling Capacity Range: 24000-28000 BTU
- Filter: Anti-Dust Filter
- Color: White
- Installation Type: Split System
- Indoor Unit Dimensions: 300 x 210 x 840 mm
- Warranty: 5 Years
