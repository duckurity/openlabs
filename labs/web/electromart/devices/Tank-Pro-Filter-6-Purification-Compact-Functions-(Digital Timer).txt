DESCRIPTION
==================================================

The Tank Pro Filter is a compact household water purification system designed to provide cleaner and better-tasting drinking water while taking up limited kitchen space. It uses BACTIBAN technology and combines six purification functions into a compact filtration system.

The filter is designed to reduce impurities such as sand, dust, rust, insects, sediments, chlorine, pesticides, organic pollutants, unpleasant odors, and unwanted taste. According to the manufacturer, the system can eliminate 99.9% of bacteria such as E. coli and salmonella, as well as 99.9% of viruses.

One of the main features is the built-in Digital Timer, which reminds the user when the cartridge needs to be replaced. The recommended cartridge replacement interval is every four months.

The Tank Pro Filter can be installed above the sink or under the sink when the appropriate connections and faucet are available. Its 360-degree rotating faucet makes it easier to position the filter around the sink.

The compact body measures approximately 29 cm x 27 cm x 10 cm, making it suitable for kitchens where counter space is limited. The filter also supports user installation and cartridge replacement without necessarily requiring a technician.
