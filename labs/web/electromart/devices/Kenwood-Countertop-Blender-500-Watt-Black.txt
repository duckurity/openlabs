DESCRIPTION
==================================================

The Kenwood BLP18.150BK is a countertop blender with a
black and grey finish, designed for everyday blending,
drinks, sauces, and general food preparation.

The blender uses a 500-watt motor and has a 2-liter bowl
capacity, providing enough space for preparing larger
quantities compared with smaller countertop blenders.

It features two speed settings along with a pulse function,
giving users greater control over blending consistency
depending on the ingredients being prepared.

The blender uses stainless steel blades and includes a
safety lock to help prevent operation when the components
are not correctly positioned.

The BLP18.150BK comes with two attachments: a grinder and
a plastic beaker. It also has non-slip feet designed to
keep the blender stable while operating.

The body is made from plastic, keeping the overall unit
relatively lightweight while maintaining a practical
countertop design.
