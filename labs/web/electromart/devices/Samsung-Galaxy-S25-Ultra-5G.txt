DESCRIPTION
==================================================

The Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra is a premium flagship smartphone
featuring a 6.9-inch Dynamic LTPO AMOLED 2X display with a
1440 x 3120 resolution, up to 120Hz adaptive refresh rate,
HDR10+, and Corning Gorilla Armor 2 protection.

The phone is powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite processor
built on a 3nm process, paired with 12GB of RAM. It is available
with 256GB, 512GB, or 1TB of internal storage and does not
support expandable storage.

The S25 Ultra features a quad-camera system on the rear,
including a 200MP main camera, a 10MP telephoto camera with
3x optical zoom, a 50MP telephoto camera with 5x optical zoom,
and a 50MP ultra-wide camera. The front camera has a 12MP
sensor. The phone supports video recording up to 8K resolution.

It comes with a 5000mAh battery supporting 45W wired fast
charging, 15W wireless charging, and 4.5W reverse wireless
charging.

The phone runs Android 15 with Samsung's One UI 7 interface
and includes an integrated S Pen. It also supports 5G,
Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 5.4, NFC, USB Type-C 3.2, Samsung DeX,
and IP68 water and dust resistance.

The device uses a titanium frame with a Gorilla Victus 2
protected glass back and weighs approximately 218 grams.

Main Specifications:
- Display: 6.9-inch Dynamic LTPO AMOLED 2X
- Resolution: 1440 x 3120 pixels
- Refresh Rate: Up to 120Hz
