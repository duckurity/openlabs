DESCRIPTION
==================================================

The LG OLED evo AI C5 is a premium 55-inch 4K OLED smart
television designed for high-quality movie watching,
streaming, and gaming.

The TV features a 4K OLED display with a resolution of
3840 x 2160 pixels. Its OLED technology provides individually
controlled pixels for deep blacks, strong contrast, and
detailed images.

The display has a native 120Hz refresh rate and supports
VRR up to 144Hz, making the TV particularly suitable for
gaming. It also supports 4K gaming at 120Hz, ALLM, G-Sync
Compatible, FreeSync Compatible, HGiG, and Dolby Vision
for Gaming.

The TV is powered by the α9 AI Processor 4K Gen8, which
provides AI-based picture processing and AI Super Upscaling
4K. It also includes AI Picture Pro, AI Brightness Control,
OLED Dynamic Tone Mapping Pro, and Filmmaker Mode.

The C5 supports Dolby Vision, HDR10, and HLG. Its OLED
display provides accurate colors and detailed highlights
while maintaining deep black levels.

The TV runs webOS 25 and includes LG's AI-powered smart
features, voice recognition, AI Search, AI Chatbot, AI
Concierge features, Multi View, Apple AirPlay, Google Cast,
and LG ThinQ integration.

Audio output is rated at 40W through a 2.2-channel speaker
system. The TV supports Dolby Atmos, AI Sound Pro, Clear
Voice Pro, AI Acoustic Tuning, and WOW Orchestra.

Connectivity includes four HDMI inputs supporting 4K 120Hz,
VRR, ALLM, QMS, and eARC. It also includes three USB 2.0
ports, Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.3, Ethernet, optical audio
output, and two RF inputs.
