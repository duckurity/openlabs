DESCRIPTION
==================================================

The Apple iPhone 17 Pro Max is a premium flagship smartphone
powered by the Apple A19 Pro processor built using 3nm
technology, paired with 12GB of RAM.

The phone features a 6.9-inch LTPO Super Retina XDR OLED
display with a 1320 x 2868 resolution, 120Hz adaptive refresh
rate, HDR10+, Dolby Vision, Always-On Display, and Ceramic
Shield 2 protection. The display can reach a peak brightness
of up to 3000 nits.

The iPhone 17 Pro Max is available with 256GB, 512GB, 1TB,
or 2TB of internal storage and does not support expandable
storage through a memory card.

It features a triple rear camera system consisting of three
48MP cameras. The main camera has optical image stabilization,
while the telephoto camera supports 4x and 8x optical-quality
zoom. The ultra-wide camera is also 48MP. The front camera
has an 18MP sensor with an SL 3D system for Face ID.

The phone supports video recording up to 4K at 120fps and
supports Dolby Vision recording. It also includes advanced
audio features with four microphones.

The iPhone 17 Pro Max has a battery capacity of approximately
4832mAh for the physical SIM version and 5088mAh for the
eSIM version. It supports 40W wired fast charging, 25W MagSafe
wireless charging, 25W Qi2 wireless charging, and 4.5W reverse
wireless charging.

The phone runs iOS 26 and supports 5G, Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6.0,
NFC, USB Type-C 3.2 Gen 2, DisplayPort, and Face ID. It also
has an IP68 rating for water and dust resistance.
