DESCRIPTION
==================================================

The Samsung U8000H is a 65-inch 4K UHD smart television
designed for everyday home entertainment, streaming,
television channels, and general multimedia use.

The TV features a 65-inch LED display with a 4K UHD
resolution of 3840 x 2160 pixels. It provides a large
viewing area suitable for living rooms and family
entertainment.

The TV includes a built-in receiver, allowing users to
receive compatible television broadcasts without requiring
a separate external receiver.

The U8000H is a smart TV with Wi-Fi and Ethernet connectivity,
allowing access to online applications and streaming services.
It also includes three HDMI ports and a USB port for connecting
external devices such as game consoles, media players, and
storage devices.

The TV comes with a remote control and is covered by a
two-year warranty according to the supplied retailer listing.

Its combination of a large 65-inch display, 4K resolution,
built-in receiver, and smart connectivity makes it suitable
for users looking for a straightforward large-screen TV
at a more accessible price.

Main Specifications:
- Display Size: 65 inches
- Display Type: LED
- Resolution: 3840 x 2160 pixels
- Resolution Type: 4K UHD
- Smart TV: Yes
- Built-In Receiver: Yes
- HDMI Ports: 3
- USB Ports: 1
- Wi-Fi: Yes
- Ethernet: Yes
- Remote Control: Included
- Series: U8000H
- Warranty: 2 Years
