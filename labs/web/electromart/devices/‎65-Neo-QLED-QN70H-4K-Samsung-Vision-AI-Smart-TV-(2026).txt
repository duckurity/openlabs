DESCRIPTION
==================================================

The Samsung Neo QLED QN70H is a premium 65-inch 4K
smart television from Samsung's 2026 lineup. It combines
Quantum Mini LED backlighting with Samsung Vision AI
features to deliver detailed images, improved contrast,
and enhanced viewing quality.

The TV features a 65-inch 4K UHD display with a resolution
of 3840 x 2160 pixels. Its Quantum Mini LED backlight is
controlled through Quantum Matrix technology, providing
more precise brightness and contrast across different
areas of the screen.

The QN70H is powered by Samsung's NQ4 AI Processor, which
uses AI-based picture and sound processing to improve
content quality and upscale compatible content to 4K.

The TV supports NQ HDR and adaptive HDR10+, along with
Motion Xcelerator, Filmmaker Mode, EyeComfort Mode, and
Color Booster Pro. It also features a wide viewing angle
and Supreme Mini LED Dimming.

For smart features, the TV runs Samsung's Tizen operating
system and supports Bixby, Samsung TV Plus, web browsing,
Apple AirPlay, Google Cast, SmartThings, Multi View, and
other connected-device features.

Gaming features include ALLM, Game Motion Plus, HGiG,
Game Bar, AI Auto Game Mode, and AI Gaming Optimizer.
The TV supports 4K gaming at up to 60Hz through its HDMI
ports.

The built-in audio system provides 20W RMS output through
a 2-channel speaker system. It supports OTS Lite,
Q-Symphony, Adaptive Sound+, Active Voice Amplifier,
and AI Sound Controller.

Connectivity includes three HDMI ports, two USB-A ports,
Ethernet, optical audio output, Wi-Fi 5, Bluetooth 5.3,
and eARC.
