DESCRIPTION
==================================================

The Mienta BL1361B is a compact countertop blender with
a black finish, designed for everyday food preparation,
smoothies, juices, sauces, and other blending tasks.

The blender is powered by a 400-watt motor and features a
1.75-liter capacity, making it suitable for preparing
moderate-sized quantities for everyday household use.

It has one main speed setting combined with a pulse
function, allowing users to control the blending process
when short bursts of additional power are needed.

The BL1361B comes with two mills that can be used for
additional food preparation tasks. Its design is intended
to be easy to clean and store, making it suitable for
kitchens with limited counter space.

The blender also includes multiple safety features and
is designed for straightforward everyday operation.
