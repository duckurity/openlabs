# Player briefing — VaultCorp

## Scenario

VaultCorp is a cloud backup SaaS platform used by small businesses to
store and manage their data backups. The company recently opened public
registration for a free trial — anyone can sign up and get their own
private workspace.

During a routine security review, an internal red team flagged the
platform as a potential target. Your job is to assess it.

## Your mission

You have been given one thing: **the URL of the VaultCorp platform**.

```
http://<target>:5000
```

Find a way to read the contents of `/app/flag.txt` on the server.

## Rules

- You are allowed to register an account (it's a free trial after all).
- You are **not** allowed to attack the infrastructure itself (DoS,
  container escape, etc.).
- The flag format is: `duck{...}`
- There is no time limit.

## Difficulty

**Hard**
