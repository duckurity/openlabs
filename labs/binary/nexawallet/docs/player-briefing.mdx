# Player briefing — NexaWallet

**Category:** Mobile Reverse Engineering
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Platform:** Android (APK provided)

NexaWallet is a small digital wallet app. You can register an account,
log in, and transfer money — up to a maximum limit of **EGP 100.00** per
transaction. Try to send more than that, and the app simply declines it:

> "Transaction declined: amount exceeds allowed limit"

Your goal is to get the app to **approve and confirm a transfer above
that limit**, and claim what it prints you when it does.

The app is not naive about this. It actively watches for signs that
it's being analyzed or tampered with, and will shut itself down if it
notices — so a plain decompile-and-edit approach, or a careless
instrumentation attempt, won't get you very far on its own.

**What you're given:**

- `NexaWallet-debug.apk` — install it on an emulator or device and play
  with it directly.

**What you need to bring:**

- A decompiler (jadx or similar) to read the app's logic.
- Frida (or equivalent dynamic instrumentation tooling) and a
  rooted/emulated Android environment capable of running it.

**Flag format:** `duck{...}`

Good luck.
